Technical Specializations

Tone-Consistent Fingerings

A new approach to fingerings on the Classical Guitar to achieve a higher speed while producing a more consistent tone for both scales and arpeggios.

Fingerstyle for Electric Guitar (Matteo Mancuso)

Classical Guitar technique of the right and left hand applied to the electric guitar the way Matteo Mancuso plays.

Two-Way Pick Slanting (Troy Grady)

Picking mechanics originally decoded by Troy Grady: alternate picking, sweeping, and legato techniques.

SWYBRYD Picking (Marshall Harrison)

Advanced right-hand mechanics blending economy picking with fingerstyle control, based on the groundbreaking concepts developed by Marshall Harrison.
